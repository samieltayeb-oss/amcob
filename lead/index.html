The Architecture of LEAD

Five Tangible Outcomes You Walk Away With

Unlike sprawling commercial conferences where attendees wander between passive presentations, LEAD is an intensive executive working summit designed around five concrete deliverables.

PILLAR 01

Personal Cognitive Assessment

Every full summit attendee receives an objective cognitive and behavioral assessment profiling your natural leadership style, communication dynamics, and decision patterns under pressure.

PILLAR 02

Enduring Peer Advisory Board

You will be placed in a revenue-matched peer cohort facilitated by certified moderators, establishing relationships that continue holding you accountable long after the summit concludes.

PILLAR 03

Deal-Making & Commercial Capital

Direct introductions to capital allocators, commercial bankers, M&A legal counsel, and potential corporate customers actively seeking vetted Muslim-owned suppliers.

PILLAR 04

Tactical Deal Closing Framework

Masterclasses led by experienced practitioners on enterprise negotiation, B2B contract structuring, and systematic sales pipelines tailored for executive teams.

PILLAR 05

Ethical & Faith-Aligned Purpose

Grounding your commercial ambition in timeless Islamic commercial ethics, fiduciary responsibility, and collective generational wealth creation for the community.

A Note From AMCOB Leadership

Why We Built This

"I have sat in enough rooms to know the difference between one that performs community and one that actually holds it."

"Most business conferences hand you a lanyard, a schedule, and a feed full of people you will never speak to again. We did not want to build that. We wanted a room where you could be a serious entrepreneur and a person of faith without translating yourself in either direction, where growing your business and growing your purpose were the same conversation, not two competing ones."

"AMCOB exists because too many of us have built successful companies while quietly wondering if success was ever really the point. The LEAD Summit is our answer to that question, compressed into 48 hours: a room built for founders who want to close deals on Saturday and still recognize themselves on Sunday."

"We did not build this to fill seats. We built it because the entrepreneurs in our community deserve one weekend a year that is entirely theirs. I hope you will be in the room."

Kashif Zubair • Founder and President, AMCOB

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before securing your seat in Anaheim.

What is LEAD Summit?

LEAD Summit is AMCOB's flagship annual event for founders, CEOs, and executives. LEAD Summit Anaheim takes place March 26 to 28, 2027 in Anaheim, bringing together 300 of the most motivated Muslim business leaders in the country.

Who should attend?

LEAD Summit is designed for Muslim founders, CEOs, and senior executives who are ready to scale, connect, and build with intention.

Do I need to be an AMCOB member to attend?

No. LEAD Summit is open to all qualified professionals. AMCOB members receive exclusive discounts.

Is the expo open to the public?

No. Only registered summit attendees and credentialed press can access the expo floor.

Are there opportunities for women entrepreneurs?

Absolutely. The AMCOB LEAD Summit welcomes Muslim women founders and executives.

How do I become a speaker or partner?

Email info@amcob.org with a short note about your work and what you would bring to the room.

Is a LEAD Summit ticket worth it?

One good introduction pays for the seat many times over , plus tools and peer relationships that outlast three days in Anaheim.

What is your refund policy?

As participation in LEAD is intentionally limited, cancellations are managed with care: Cancellations made on or before January 1 are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations made between January 2 and February 15 are eligible for a 50% refund. After February 15, participation is non-refundable.
