ADVERTISING TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Effective for all newsletter and website advertising placements with AMCOB.

These Terms and Conditions govern all paid advertising placements across AMCOB's newsletter and website, amcob.org. By submitting an advertising request, the advertiser agrees to the terms set out below.

1. Who Can Advertise
AMCOB advertising is open to businesses, sponsors, and organizations whose offerings are relevant to the Muslim business community and consistent with AMCOB's core values, including Amanah (trust), Excellence, Community, and Halal Business Practices. AMCOB reserves the right to decline any advertiser whose content or business does not align with these values.

2. Advertising Policy: Shared Values, Shared Vision
We carefully select businesses whose principles align with ours. To preserve the trust and integrity of the AMCOB community, prospective businesses must:
A. Uphold human rights and global justice with zero tolerance for discrimination or oppression.
B. Demonstrate ethical business practices, including fair labor, environmental stewardship, and sound governance.
C. Maintain political neutrality on divisive or discriminatory issues.
D. Foster inclusive, equitable workplaces with strong diversity and empowerment policies.
E. Support economic and social empowerment initiatives aligned with AMCOB's vision.
F. Commit to transparency and integrity throughout the business relationship.

3. Ad Slot Availability
A. Newsletter placements are limited to a maximum of three paid slots per monthly edition, confirmed on a first come, first served basis.
B. Website placements run for a full calendar month once live.

4. Content Requirements
A. All advertisements must align with AMCOB's mission and values. AMCOB reserves the right to reject or remove any advertisement that conflicts with them.
B. The advertiser is solely responsible for the accuracy of all claims made within their advertisement content.

5. Formatting and Technical Specifications
Exact formatting specifications, including dimensions, file type, and character limits, will be provided upon booking confirmation. Advertisements that do not meet the supplied specifications may be delayed until corrected and resubmitted.

6. Submission and Approval
A. The advertiser must submit finalized advertisement content, including all text, images, and links, before the start of the advertising period.
B. AMCOB will review all submissions for compliance with formatting, content, and mission alignment prior to placement.
C. Placement is confirmed only once content is approved and payment has been received in full.

7. Payment
Full payment is required to confirm a booking. Bookings are confirmed on a first come, first served basis and are not held or reserved without payment.

8. Cancellations and Refunds
Cancellations must be submitted at least fifteen days before the scheduled newsletter send date or website placement start date to receive a full refund. Cancellations made after this window are not eligible for a refund.

9. Timing
A. Newsletter. Sent once per month; the exact send date is communicated upon booking confirmation.
B. Website. Placements go live within three business days of booking confirmation.

10. Reporting
Open and click performance data is shared with the advertiser after each newsletter send.

11. Removal and Termination
AMCOB reserves the right to remove any advertisement at any time for non-compliance with these terms or misalignment with AMCOB's mission and values. In cases of material violation, this may occur without refund.

12. Intellectual Property and Liability
A. The advertiser confirms they hold all necessary rights, including copyright and trademark, to the content submitted.
B. The advertiser grants AMCOB a license to display the advertisement content across the agreed medium or mediums for the duration of the booking.
C. AMCOB is not liable for indirect or consequential damages arising from the placement, delay, or removal of an advertisement.
D. The advertiser agrees to indemnify AMCOB against any claims arising from the content of their advertisement.

13. Term and Renewal
Each booking covers a single advertising period, defined as one monthly newsletter send or one calendar month of website placement. Renewal requires a new submission, approval, and payment following this same process.
