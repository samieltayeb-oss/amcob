The AMCOB Lifetime Founding Circle

Honoring those whose character, service, and leadership define our business community.

The AMCOB Lifetime Founding Circle is a permanent honor reserved for a very small group of individuals whose lives and work reflect integrity, excellence, and a deep commitment to the economic empowerment of the Muslim community.

These individuals are not simply members of AMCOB, they are stewards of its mission.

Each Founding Circle Member has been personally invited based on:

Proven character and ethical leadership

Longstanding devotion to serving the Muslim community

Distinguished professional and entrepreneurial accomplishments

A belief in building institutions that outlast individuals

Members of the Lifetime Founding Circle receive lifetime access to all AMCOB offerings and are permanently recognized on this page as part of AMCOB's founding legacy.

This circle exists to honor legacy, not status - and responsibility, not privilege
