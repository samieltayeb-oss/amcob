About CONSELARA, LLC

Conselara was founded in 2014 by health IT professionals who had spent years at the intersection of federal health research and technology delivery. We saw agencies investing heavily in health IT while the systems often did not reflect what the research behind them required. The gap was not technical. It was a matter of understanding.

We built Conselara around that understanding. Today our team brings together federally funded health research management, cloud and AI engineering, digital health interoperability, and stakeholder adoption. We work as a prime, a subcontractor, and a teaming partner, and we treat clients and collaborators as long-term relationships, not one-time engagements.
