About the Allied Muslim Chamber of Business

AMCOB was founded to establish durable economic institutions, foster confidential peer mentorship, and unlock cross-border commercial opportunities for Muslim founders and business executives.

Our Purpose and Genesis

The Allied Muslim Chamber of Business was established to bridge a critical structural gap in the commercial landscape. While Muslim business owners in the United States, Canada, and global hubs create substantial enterprise value, they frequently operate in isolation without institutional representation, structured peer accountability, or institutional capital channels.

AMCOB provides the formal chamber architecture that connects these enterprises. We focus on four foundational pillars: Growth, Accountability, Isolation Relief, and Networking.

Today, the chamber represents over 400 verified enterprises spanning healthcare systems, enterprise software, commercial real estate, logistics, legal counsel, and manufacturing.

The Chamber Charter

  • ✓ Fiduciary Trust: Uncompromising ethical integrity in all business dealings and commercial transactions.
  • ✓ Peer Confidentiality: Strict non-disclosure and trust during closed-door advisory boards.
  • ✓ Economic Solidarity: Active preference for verified member suppliers, contractors, and partners.
  • ✓ Intergenerational Capital: Creating institutional business infrastructure that endures across generations.

Executive Message

A Message from the Chief Executive

"Running a successful business is one of the most demanding callings an individual can undertake. When entrepreneurs reach critical revenue thresholds, the challenges shift from execution to governance, capital strategy, and organizational endurance. AMCOB exists to ensure that no Muslim founder walks this path alone. By combining rigorous peer accountability with genuine brotherhood and ethical alignment, we are building a commercial bedrock for generations to come."

Kashif Zubair | Founder & CEO, Allied Muslim Chamber of Business
