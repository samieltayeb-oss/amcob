Your confidential, non-competing board of directors. Matched strictly by verified revenue stage, guided by professional facilitators, and bound by strict confidentiality.

The Executive Advisory Table

A trusted environment of 8 to 12 verified Muslim business owners solving complex corporate growth, hiring, margin, and governance challenges without conflict of interest.

Apply for Cohort Placement

A Private Board of Directors for Founders

Most founders cannot share their deepest operational, financial, or governance anxieties with their staff, investors, or family. Peer Advisory Groups solve executive isolation by assembling a curated table of 8 to 12 seasoned Muslim entrepreneurs who understand your scale.

Every group meets once each month for three focused hours. Sessions follow a structured agenda: personal check-ins, executive accountability scorecards, and deep-dive challenge processing for two members per meeting.

Competitors are strictly excluded. All proceedings operate under strict non-disclosure commitments.

The 5 Key Governance Rules

  • 01 Revenue Verification: Members must verify tax or financial statements to ensure true stage peer matching.
  • 02 Zero Competitors: No direct competitors are ever placed in the same cohort.
  • 03 Confidentiality Commitment: Complete confidentiality is required from every participant.
  • 04 Attendance Commitment: Minimum 80% annual meeting attendance is strictly enforced.
  • 05 Experience Sharing: Advice is offered through personal case experience rather than theoretical opinion.
