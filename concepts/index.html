The higher you climb, the smaller the room gets.

Peer Advisory Groups

Confidential counsel with peers who match your scale

AMCOB peer advisory groups group business leaders with non-competing peers who operate at a similar revenue scale. Groups meet monthly in confidential sessions led by a trained facilitator to address operating decisions, governance, and executive leadership.

Scaling Enterprises Peer Advisory

Eligible business revenue: $5 Million to $25 Million
$3,850
per year / official tier

Focus and operating challenges

Managing mid-market growth, recruiting executive leadership, building sustainable governance structures, and preparing for regional or international expansion.

Meeting rhythm and structure

Closed monthly 3-hour roundtable with 8 to 10 non-competing peers. Guided by a certified facilitator with strict confidentiality.

Included chamber benefits

Full AMCOB Connect client referral access included, priority registration for bilateral trade missions, and executive retreat access.

Application and verification

Confidential revenue verification review followed by a placement interview to ensure cohort alignment and avoid commercial conflicts.

Membership Architecture

All verified AMCOB membership and advisory programs

Participation is structured according to enterprise size and commercial objectives. All fees are in US Dollars and billed on an annual basis.

Program Eligibility criteria Program inclusions Annual fee Next step

AMCOB Connect B2B companies seeking structured client referrals Structured referral meetings, portal customer matching, directory listing $850.00 / year
PAG: Emerging Enterprises Annual revenue under $1 Million Monthly confidential peer cohort with full AMCOB Connect included $1,925.00 / year
PAG: Booming Enterprises Annual revenue $1 Million to $5 Million Monthly peer mastermind, certified facilitation, executive accountability $2,750.00 / year
PAG: Scaling Enterprises Annual revenue $5 Million to $25 Million Mid-market CEO council, executive hiring, M&A discussions, retreat priority $3,850.00 / year
