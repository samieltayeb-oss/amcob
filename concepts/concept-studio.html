Rigorous comparison of the three distinct creative directions. These represent structural, editorial, and experiential differences, not superficial color or font swaps.

Historic global trading house, bilateral civic institution, multi-generational mercantile legacy. High-velocity executive intelligence network, zero bureaucracy, actionable peer takeaways.
