AMCOB Broadcast

Rethinking Where to Incorporate #AMCOB

Right now, Canada isn't as conducive for business owners or investors. That's why we started there but have since shifted our focus to Panama. We previously helped clients in the GCC as well, places like Dubai and free zones, but because of current geopolitics, we're focusing on Panama for now. We can also help with incorporation in Hong Kong. One major benefit of Panama is citizenship eligibility after five years, without having to actually live there. Similar to the Portugal Golden Visa, where an investment can qualify people for citizenship in 10 years without residing there. Panama offers the same, along with a territorial tax system, much like Hong Kong or the Marshall Islands. Anything earned outside the country, whether crypto or trading, isn't taxed, because it falls outside Panamanian borders. That's the foundation for anyone looking to relocate. #Panama #OffshoreIncorporation #TerritorialTax #Relocation #GlobalCitizen #AMCOB

Where the Value Is Right Now #GlobalInvesting

There are new diamonds in the rough in Central and South America, places like Panama, alongside the US, which I'm still bullish on. I also like Portugal, Malta, and Cyprus because they're not oversaturated. A lot of markets have entry points or costs that are just too high. I like value. If I were in the financial markets, I'd be looking for equities that offer real value, and that's exactly how I think about these countries too. From an immigration perspective, Americans tend to gravitate toward the Mediterranean, though they're usually not chasing a second passport as much. Canadians, on the other hand, are looking for something more tax friendly, with good weather and good safety. #GlobalInvesting #Relocation #SecondPassport #Panama #Portugal #Malta
